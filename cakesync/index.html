CakeSync

Your cakes, quoted, tracked and posted.

CakeSync is an Android app for home bakers, cake decorators and small bakeries. It finds the cake photos in your camera roll, sorts them, and turns them into posts, a menu and tracked orders, all on your phone.

What it does

  • Finds and sorts your cakes. One scan finds every cake photo and video on your phone. The scan runs privately on the device, skips photos with people in them and near-duplicates, and saves your cakes into dated folders in your own gallery.
  • Gets them ready to post. Photo studio, filters, collages and reels, with your logo, business name and price, shared to Instagram or WhatsApp.
  • Keeps track of orders. Send customers an order form, turn their reply into an order, schedule it in your calendar, and keep a menu with your prices.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, CakeSync uploads the cake photos and videos you selected to folders it creates in your own Drive, so you have a backup.

CakeSync asks only for permission to see and manage the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot read anything else in your Drive, and nothing is sent to the developer. Drive backup is off until you turn it on, and you can disconnect it at any time in the app's Settings.

Your data

CakeSync has no account and no server of its own. Your photos, orders, menu and settings stay on your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
